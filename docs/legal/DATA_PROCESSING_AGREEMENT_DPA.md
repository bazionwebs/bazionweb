# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — Bazionweb

**Document Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026  
**Applicable Regulations:** Section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (Act No. 24 of 2019) & Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

---

## 1. Scope, Purpose & Execution

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Bazionweb Terms of Service and Master Hosting Agreement entered into between:

1. **The Client** (acting as **Data Controller**), whose identity and contact details are set out in the applicable Service Order or registration profile; and
2. **Bazionweb** (acting as **Data Processor**), a cloud hosting and application deployment provider organized under the laws of Kenya, with registered offices in Nairobi, Kenya.

This DPA applies whenever Bazionweb processes personal data hosted within the Client's databases, web applications, containers, or backup snapshots in connection with the performance of hosting services.

---

## 2. Subject Matter & Nature of Processing

1. **Subject Matter:** The provision of server infrastructure, Docker application containers, database storage, reverse proxy traffic routing, automated SSL termination, and off-site backup storage.
2. **Duration:** The term of the underlying Hosting Service Agreement plus thirty (30) days for post-termination data export.
3. **Nature & Purpose:** Storage, computation, network routing, maintenance, and backup of customer-managed web applications.
4. **Categories of Data:** Any personal data uploaded, collected, or generated by the Client's applications (including customer records, transaction details, visitor IP addresses, user accounts, and communications).
5. **Categories of Data Subjects:** The Client's end-users, customers, employees, or website visitors.

---

## 3. Obligations of the Data Processor (Bazionweb)

In strict adherence to Section 30 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, Bazionweb covenants and agrees to:

1. **Documented Instructions:** Process Client personal data solely in accordance with the documented instructions of the Client (including instructions relating to workload provisioning, resource limits, and backup scheduling), unless required to do so by applicable Kenyan or international law.
2. **Staff Confidentiality:** Ensure that all personnel, engineers, and contractors authorized to access infrastructure or customer systems are committed to strict contractual confidentiality and data security obligations.
3. **Technical & Organizational Security (Section 41):** Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
   - Linux cgroups v2 resource quotas (`cpuLimit`, `memoryLimit`) and unprivileged container execution (`UID 1001`);
   - Encryption of all external traffic in transit using TLS 1.3 / TLS 1.2;
   - Client-side AES-256 encryption of off-site backup snapshots prior to transmission;
   - Network isolation using dedicated Docker bridge networks, preventing inter-tenant cross-talk;
   - Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and SSH key authentication for all administrative infrastructure.
4. **Sub-Processor Governance:** Not engage any sub-processor without maintaining an updated list of authorized providers and binding each sub-processor to contractual data protection terms at least as stringent as those set out in this DPA.
5. **Assistance with Data Subject Rights:** Taking into account the nature of processing, provide technical assistance to enable the Controller to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Section 26 of the Kenya DPA (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Objection, and Portability).
6. **Breach Notification SLA (Section 43):** Notify the Client in writing without undue delay, and in any event within **forty-eight (48) hours** of confirming any unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or personal data breach affecting Client workloads. Provide detailed incident telemetry to assist the Client in notifying the ODPC within the statutory seventy-two (72) hour window.
7. **Audit & Compliance Demonstration:** Make available to the Controller all reasonable information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and submit to reasonable security audits conducted by an independent certified auditor upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice.
8. **Data Return & Deletion:** Upon termination of services, permit the Client to export all hosted databases, code, and files for thirty (30) days. Following the expiration of the export window, securely and irrevocably delete all production container volumes, databases, and backup snapshots, unless statutory law mandates retention.

---

## 4. Obligations of the Data Controller (The Client)

The Client covenants and warrants that:
1. It possesses all necessary legal authority, user consents, and lawful bases required under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 to collect, process, and transfer personal data to Bazionweb for hosting.
2. It maintains an updated privacy notice informing its users that data will be hosted on managed infrastructure and may be stored in certified foreign data centers.
3. It will configure application-level credentials, secure passwords, and software dependencies responsibly, applying security updates to its own custom application code and CMS plugins.

## 6. International Data Transfers

1. Personal data processed under this DPA may be transferred to and stored in data centers located in the European Union (Netherlands, Germany) or the United States.
2. In compliance with Section 48 and 49 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, transfers outside Kenya are executed on the basis of:
   - Reciprocal adequacy determinations where the destination country affords comparable protections;
   - Appropriate contractual safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses);
   - Strong client-side encryption ensuring that off-site backups are unreadable to storage providers without cryptographic keys held exclusively by Bazionweb.

---

## 7. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Kenya. Any dispute arising under or in connection with this agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of Nairobi, Kenya, without prejudice to a data subject's statutory right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).
