## 3. Standardized Service Definition Framework

Every service in the BazionWeb catalogue conforms to a standardized specification format. This ensures consistency across client proposals, website marketing pages, and team delivery workflows:

1. **Service Name & Category**
2. **What Is It?** (Clear, non-technical plain English summary)
3. **Who Is It For?** (Target organization size, archetype, and readiness)
4. **What Problem Does It Solve?** (Commercial, operational, or technical pain point)
5. **What Is Included?** (Explicit checklist of deliverables)
6. **What Is Not Included?** (Out-of-scope boundaries that protect margin)
7. **Deliverables & Milestones** (Concrete tangible artifacts received by client)
8. **Estimated Delivery Timeline** (Standard working days/weeks)
9. **Commercial Starting Price** (Kenya KES + International USD)
10. **Optional Add-ons** (Up-sell pathways)
11. **Third-Party Costs** (Disclosures of pass-through software/cloud fees)
12. **Client Responsibilities** (Content, assets, approvals, domain access)
13. **Post-Launch Warranty & Support** (Included defect warranty window)
14. **Primary Call to Action** (Direct conversion goal)

## 4. Family 01: Websites

### 4.1 Launch (Starter Website)
* **What is it?** A streamlined, fast, high-converting 5-page responsive website designed to establish an immediate, credible web presence.
* **Who is it for?** Startups, sole practitioners, independent consultants, local trades, and small businesses launching their first digital footprint.
* **What problem does it solve?** Invisibility on Google, lack of credibility, reliance on social media pages that do not convert, slow and unsecure DIY website builders.
* **What's included:**
  * Up to 5 core pages: Home, About, Services, Gallery/Portfolio, Contact
  * Mobile-first responsive layout (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Android, iOS)
  * Interactive Contact Form with email dispatch and anti-spam verification
  * Instant WhatsApp floating click-to-chat integration
  * Google Maps business pin embed and social media profile linking
  * Technical SEO foundation: meta titles, descriptions, OpenGraph social cards
  * SSL HTTPS certificate configuration
  * Google Analytics 4 (GA4) integration
  * Production deployment to high-speed CDN
  * 1 comprehensive design revision round
  * 14-day post-launch technical defect warranty
* **What's not included:** Ongoing content authoring, copy typing from handwritten documents, commercial photo shoots, e-commerce cart/checkout, dynamic database apps.
* **Timeline:** 5–10 business days.
* **Pricing:** KSh 25,000 (Kenya) / $495 (International).
* **CTA:** *Get a Free Assessment / Launch My Website*

### 4.2 Business Website (Most Popular)

* **What problem does it solve?** Outdated brand image, inability to edit website content internally, weak search engine rankings, poor mobile responsiveness, low lead conversion.
* **What's included:**
  * Up to 10 structured pages with custom UI/UX design
  * Headless / intuitive CMS for client self-service content editing
