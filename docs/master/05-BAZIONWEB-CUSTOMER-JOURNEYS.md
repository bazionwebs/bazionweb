## 1. Architectural Philosophy: From Generic Service to Vertical Solution

A generic web agency says: *"We build WordPress websites."*  
BazionWeb solves domain-specific organizational bottlenecks:
* For a church, we eliminate chaotic manual tithe tracking and disjointed WhatsApp announcements.
* For a school, we replace error-prone paper grade sheets with a secure student information portal.
* For an SME, we convert a stagnant brochure website into an automated lead-generating asset.

By tailoring the digital experience through the **Discover → Understand → Configure → Request → Contract → Build → Launch → Manage → Grow** pipeline, BazionWeb dramatically increases conversion and client lifetime value (LTV).

## 2. Vertical 01: SMEs & Growing Businesses

### 2.1 Baseline State & Core Pain Points
* **Current State:** Stagnant 3-page website built years ago; mobile layout is broken; not ranking on Google for high-intent local queries; rely solely on WhatsApp and manual Instagram DMs.
* **Pain Points:** Lost revenue to competitors with modern websites; no inbound lead capture after hours; website looks amateurish; fear of being locked into a slow, expensive agency.

### 2.2 Recommended Package & Add-Ons
* **Core Package:** **Business Website** (KSh 50,000 / $1,250).
* **High-Value Add-Ons:** `ADDON-WEB-BLOG` (News engine), `ADDON-WEB-FORM` (Multi-step quote request), `ADDON-OPS-SEOA` (Local SEO setup).
* **Care Plan:** **Care Business** (KSh 10,000/mo / $150/mo).

### 2.3 End-to-End Journey Map
1. **Discover:** Discovers BazionWeb via Google search ("professional web design Kenya") or LinkedIn case study.
2. **Understand:** Reads the `/solutions/smes` page highlighting real lead generation, fast page speeds, and verified client ownership.
3. **Configure:** Uses the `/quote` configurator; chooses "Business Website" + "SEO" + "Lead Form". Receives an instant itemized estimate: KSh 62,500.
4. **Request:** Submits the configuration with contact details.
5. **Contract:** Receives formal BazionWeb proposal with scope, milestone payment terms (50/50), and 100% ownership guarantee within 2 hours. Signs digitally.
6. **Build:** 15-day sprint with milestone review at day 7.
7. **Launch:** Production deployment with Google Search Console verification.
8. **Manage:** Transitions to Care Business retainer for backups and monthly SEO audits.
9. **Grow:** BazionWeb identifies high-converting keywords and helps launch a targeted landing page.

## 3. Vertical 02: Churches & Ministries

### 3.1 Baseline State & Core Pain Points
* **Current State:** Facebook page only or an outdated website with dead links; manual collection of tithes/offerings with reconciliation discrepancies; fragmented member communication across dozens of unorganized WhatsApp groups.
* **Pain Points:** Inability for diaspora members to give easily via card/M-Pesa; lack of central member database; sermons and event recordings buried on social platforms.

### 3.2 Recommended Package & Add-Ons
* **Core Package:** **Professional Website** (KSh 85,000 / $2,250) or **Church Management System (ChMS)** (From KSh 200,000 / $5,000).
* **High-Value Add-Ons:** `ADDON-ECO-MPESA` (Automated tithes & offerings reconciliation), `ADDON-ECO-SMS` (Africa's Talking bulk SMS announcements), Audio/Video Sermon Archive.
* **Care Plan:** **Care Business** (KSh 10,000/mo / $150/mo).

### 3.3 End-to-End Journey Map
1. **Discover:** Pastor or media administrator lands on `/solutions/churches`.
2. **Understand:** Reads how other ministries automated online giving and central member communication.
3. **Configure:** Selects "Church Platform" + "M-Pesa Giving" + "Sermon Media Player".
4. **Request:** Books a Digital Assessment call with a BazionWeb solutions consultant.
5. **Contract:** Governance board reviews proposal; terms include church data sovereignty and safe diaspora payment processing.
6. **Build:** Custom UI reflecting the ministry’s vision; integration with Safaricom Daraja STK Push for instant tithe acknowledgement SMS.
7. **Launch:** Sunday launch live-stream demonstration and pastor announcement.
8. **Manage & Grow:** Ongoing uptime monitoring for Sunday peak streaming; monthly giving analytics reports.

## 4. Vertical 03: Schools & Educational Institutions

### 4.1 Baseline State & Core Pain Points
* **Current State:** Basic brochure site with out-of-date term dates; physical fee deposit slips lost in queues; paper report cards; chaotic admission season phone lines.
* **Pain Points:** High administrative workload on bursars and teachers; delayed fee reconciliation; parent frustration regarding exam grades and school announcements.

### 4.2 Recommended Package & Add-Ons
* **Core Package:** **School Management System (UMS/SIS)** (From KSh 250,000 / $7,500) + Public Portal.
* **High-Value Add-Ons:** `ADDON-SYS-RBAC` (Teacher/Bursar/Parent/Student permissions), `ADDON-SYS-PDF` (Automated report card generation), `ADDON-ECO-MPESA` (Fee payment ledger).
* **Care Plan:** **Care Professional** (KSh 20,000/mo / $300/mo) with guaranteed term-start peak capacity.

### 4.3 End-to-End Journey Map
1. **Discover:** School Director searches for "school portal and management system Kenya".
2. **Understand:** Reviews `/solutions/education` with live screenshots of admissions, grade books, and fee ledgers.
3. **Configure:** Submits discovery request with student enrollment count and campus numbers.
4. **Request & Discovery:** Paid Discovery Sprint (KSh 35,000 credited to project) delivering data model and parent workflows.
5. **Contract:** Milestone contract approved by Board of Governors.
6. **Build:** Secure Next.js 14 frontend with NestJS / PostgreSQL backend; parent SMS alerts on attendance and fee receipts.
7. **Launch:** On-site administrator and teacher training; phased rollout by grade.
8. **Manage & Grow:** 99.9% uptime during fee payment cycles; addition of e-learning modules in Phase 2.

* Multi-currency donation pipelines (USD, EUR, GBP, KES).
