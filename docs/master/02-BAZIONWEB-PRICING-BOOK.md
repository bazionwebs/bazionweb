## 1. Commercial Philosophy & Dual-Market Strategy

BazionWeb rejects both low-ball commodity pricing and arbitrary currency exchange rates. We operate with two distinct, commercially defensible market tracks:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┐
│         KENYA DOMESTIC MARKET         │         INTERNATIONAL MARKET          │
├───────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Currency: Kenyan Shillings (KES)    │ • Currency: United States Dollar (USD)│
│ • Target: Local SMEs, Churches, NGOs, │ • Target: Overseas Businesses,        │
│   Schools, Startups, Professionals    │   Institutions, Funded Startups       │
│ • Local Payment: M-Pesa STK, Bank EFT │ • Payment: Card (Stripe/IntaSend),    │
│ • Regional Context & Business Hours   │   Wise, International Wire (SWIFT)    │
│ • Competitive positioning against     │ • Competitive against Western agencies│
│   Nairobi agency rate benchmarks      │   while providing senior engineering  │
└───────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Critical Rule:** The website and commercial proposals must **never** perform live math conversion (e.g. "KSh 50,000 / $387"). International clients are purchasing a higher-touch service track with formal discovery documentation, international contract agreements, time-zone aligned support, and dedicated project management.

## 4. Payment Terms & Billing Schedules

### 4.1 Standard Projects (< KSh 100,000 / < $3,000)
1. **50% Initial Deposit:** Required to initiate discovery, design prototypes, and schedule engineering.
2. **50% Final Balance:** Due upon completion of QA and client sign-off, **prior** to production deployment and DNS handover.

### 4.2 Large & Milestone Projects (> KSh 100,000 / > $3,000)
* **40% Milestone 1:** Discovery, UI/UX clickable prototypes, and architecture approval.
* **30% Milestone 2:** Core feature development and staging environment alpha release.
* **20% Milestone 3:** Feature freeze, beta testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
* **10% Milestone 4:** Final deployment, production launch, admin handover training, and warranty commencement.

## 5. Contractual Policies & Client Guarantees

### 5.1 100% Client Ownership Guarantee
Unless an explicit SaaS subscription contract specifies otherwise, **the client owns 100% of their digital assets**:
* Domain name registration (registered under client credentials).
* All website and application source code.
* All brand content, media, copy, and database records.
* Full administrative access and master credentials upon final invoice clearance.
* BazionWeb **never** withholds a client's domain name or business assets.

### 5.2 Third-Party Costs Pass-Through Rule
Third-party platform subscriptions, cloud compute, SMS credits, domain renewal fees, paid API keys, and payment processor transaction percentages (e.g. M-Pesa, Stripe, IntaSend, AWS, Twilio, Google Workspace) are the direct financial responsibility of the client. They are never bundled into one-time engineering fees unless explicitly itemized on the quotation.

### 5.3 Revision Policy vs. Scope Creep
* A **Revision** is an iteration or visual adjustment on an agreed feature, mockup, or layout within the signed scope.
* A **Change Request** is the addition of new features, extra pages, unlisted third-party APIs, or structural redesigns after prototype approval.
* Change requests are scoped, costed at standard rates, and approved in writing before engineering starts.

### 5.4 Rush Project Expedited Delivery Rates
* **Priority Delivery (+25% surcharge):** 30% reduction in delivery calendar; dedicated evening engineering blocks.
* **Emergency Rush Delivery (+50% surcharge):** 50% reduction in delivery calendar; weekend on-call engineering.
* Rush engagements are subject to senior engineer capacity availability.

### 5.5 Quotation Validity
All official quotations generated by the BazionWeb Project Configurator or sales team remain firm for **30 calendar days** from date of issue.
